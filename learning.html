Deep Learning in 100 Hours

A personal one-month review plan designed around my background in mathematics, statistics and data science. It begins with a simple MLP and continues through Transformers, LoRA, QLoRA and an introduction to medical visual question answering.

Purpose

I created these materials to review topics I have encountered before, connect the mathematical ideas to complete PyTorch workflows, and build a stronger foundation for the next stage of my research. The simpler material is reviewed briefly rather than skipped, while more time is given to Transformers and parameter-efficient fine-tuning.

One-month path

  1. PyTorch foundationsMLPs, losses, full backpropagation, training loops, CNNs, RNN/GRU and checkpoints.
  2. Transformers and language modelsTokens, attention, Transformer blocks, Tiny GPT and the main LLaMA components.
  3. Fine-tuning and question answeringLoRA, QLoRA memory reasoning, QA evaluation and a controlled rank comparison.
  4. Vision and multimodal learningViT, CLIP, vision-language fusion and a lightweight PathVQA-style capstone.
