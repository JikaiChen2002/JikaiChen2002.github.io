I'm a PhD researcher in the Division of Informatics, Imaging and Data Sciences at The University of Manchester, supervised by Dr Mobarak Hoque.

I work on medical AI: deep learning methods for multimodal medical imaging, from radiological scans (CT and MRI) to digital pathology slides. My current project focuses on appendiceal cancer. I came to medical imaging from mathematics and statistics, and I like problems where a clean model of the data meets a messy clinical reality.

Where is the appendix?

My current project is on appendiceal cancer. The appendix is a small, finger-like pouch attached to the caecum, where the small intestine meets the large intestine, in the lower right of the abdomen. Cancers here are rare and are often found only after the appendix has been removed for suspected appendicitis, which is one reason careful reading of CT scans matters.
